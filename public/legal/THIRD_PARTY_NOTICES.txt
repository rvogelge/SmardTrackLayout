SmardTrackLayout - Third-party notices

Track geometry: XTrackCAD parameter libraries
---------------------------------------------

The track pieces in SmardTrackLayout (shapes, end points and part numbers)
come from the parameter libraries (.xtp files) of the XTrackCAD project:

    XTrackCAD - Model Railroad CAD
    Project:  https://sourceforge.net/p/xtrkcad-fork/
    Source:   https://sourceforge.net/p/xtrkcad-fork/xtrkcad/ci/default/tree/app/lib/params/
    Website:  http://www.xtrackcad.org/

Files included in this distribution (unmodified copies):

    HO-Marklin C-Track.xtp   (Märklin C-Track, HO; English names)
    mrklnhoc-de.xtp          (Märklin C-Gleis, HO; German names)

The complete, editable source of these files is the files themselves.
They can also be obtained from the XTrackCAD source repository linked
above.

All other libraries are not included. SmardTrackLayout downloads them on
request from the XTrackCAD repository on SourceForge and keeps a local
cache. Files that are not UTF-8 are converted to UTF-8 for display. Their
content is otherwise not changed.

SmardTrackLayout reads the parameter files as data with its own parser.
It does not contain XTrackCAD program code.
