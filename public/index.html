How to use

  • Place: click a piece in the library. It attaches to the highlighted open end (or appears in the centre). You can also drag it onto the canvas.
  • Flip end (Tab): attach the last placed piece by its other endpoint, e.g. to make a curve bend right instead of left.
  • Connect: drag a piece close to an open end and it snaps on. Open ends are orange; the active one (where the next piece goes) is larger and blue. Click an open end to make it active.
  • Select: click a piece; Shift+click adds to the selection; double-click selects everything connected; Shift+drag on empty space draws a selection box.
  • Rotate: R / Shift+R by the chosen step. Delete: Del.
  • View: drag empty space to pan, mouse wheel to zoom, F to fit.
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z, redo Ctrl+Shift+Z. Your work is also autosaved in this browser.

Track geometry comes from the XTrackCAD parameter library on SourceForge (xtrkcad-fork).
