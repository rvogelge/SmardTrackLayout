How to use

  • Place: click a piece in the library. It attaches to the highlighted open end (or appears in the centre). You can also drag it onto the canvas.
  • Flip end (Tab): attach the last placed piece by its other endpoint, e.g. to make a curve bend right instead of left.
  • Connect: drag a piece close to an open end and it snaps on. Open ends are orange; the active one (where the next piece goes) is larger and blue. Click an open end to make it active.
  • Mark: click an item; Shift+click adds or removes. Shift+drag on empty space marks an area: left→right marks what is completely inside, right→left also what it touches. Double-click a track piece to mark everything connected. Click empty space or press Esc to unmark.
  • Rotate: drag the round handle above the selection (snaps to the angle field; hold Shift for free rotation), or R / Shift+R by the angle in the toolbar (type any value). Ctrl+A then rotate turns the whole design. Delete: Del.
  • Draw (toolbar on the canvas): line L, polyline P, rectangle B, circle O, dimension D, text T, back to select V/Esc. Points snap to the grid, to corners and to track ends. While drawing, type a length in mm (rectangle: 2400x1200) and press Enter; Shift locks to 15° steps. Select a single drawing to drag its corner handles; double-click text to edit it.
  • Rectangle size: mark a rectangle and type Size X / Size Y in the panel at the top right (Enter jumps into it). Rectangles: drag a side line to change only the size in that direction, drag a corner to change both (it stays a rectangle), drag inside a marked rectangle to move it. Their dimensions follow.
  • Save (Ctrl+S) writes to the file the layout was opened from or last saved to; Save as… (Ctrl+Shift+S) chooses a new file. Open… (Ctrl+O) loads a file. (Browsers other than Chrome/Edge can only download, so there Save works like Save as….) Your current work is also kept automatically between sessions.
  • Move the workspace: drag on empty space, or swipe with two fingers on the trackpad (also right/middle-drag, or Space+drag while drawing). Zoom: mouse wheel, pinch on the trackpad, +/−, or the buttons bottom right; F fits everything.
  • Copy / paste: Ctrl+C, Ctrl+X, Ctrl+V copy, cut and paste the marked items (track and drawings; a rectangle brings its dimensions along). Paste puts them at the mouse pointer. Ctrl+D duplicates in place, slightly offset. Works between tabs/windows too. (On a Mac: ⌘ instead of Ctrl.)
  • Undo: Ctrl+Z, redo Ctrl+Shift+Z. Your work is also autosaved in this browser.

Track geometry comes from the XTrackCAD parameter library on SourceForge (xtrkcad-fork).
